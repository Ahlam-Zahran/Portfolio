Featured project

NeuroSeekers → Neurender

Software that rebuilds the image a person is looking at from their fMRI brain scan, with every result tested against chance. Neurender is the product that grew out of my graduation project at the University of Petra, built first for research labs that need brain-decoding results they can audit.

Read the brain7T fMRI from the Natural Scenes Dataset: 26,688 voxels per image, one participant at a time.
Decode meaning & layoutRidge-regression decoders predict what a scene is about and where things are.
Generate the imageStable Diffusion paints the picture from those two signals in about 30 seconds.
Prove itScored against chance, a measured noise ceiling and 1,000 shuffled controls. Every run is traceable.
82.1%two-way identification accuracy, 982 held-out images (chance 50%)
p < 0.001against 1,000 shuffled controls; the real score was never reached
~30 sper image on a warm system

Early-stage research system: results are for one participant in a public dataset. Next: validation on standard hospital scanners and a published verification standard for brain-decoding claims.
